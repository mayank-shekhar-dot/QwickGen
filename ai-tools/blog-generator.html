About Blog Generator

Introduction

A Blog is a kind of website or webpage where a person writes regularly or spontaneously about the topics which interests them, usually it contains the photographs, content, links to connect them to the other source (websites) which may influence them or is of their kind / interest. Also a blog is a written text - based content rather than that of a vlog which is a video log where content is shared or delivered through a recorded video. It may be carried out by anyone such as commercial enterprises, individuals, institutions, organisations, etc.

Key Features of Blogging

  • Tone - Articles or the blog post should maintain the pace of writing in such a manner that it should sound polite, friendly and easy to understand. It can contain a personal or informal style of interaction.
  • Words - The blog post contains the precise and concise information within it making the blog look attention seeking to the reader.
  • Updates - Unlike other websites which keep a distance for long duration instead of being regular, a blog features fresh and daily basis or regular updates to keep in touch with the users while maintaining the work on a frequent schedule.
  • Comments - At the end most of the blog features a section known as 'comment section' which provides a service to the reader to share feedback and have interaction directly with the author or the user involved within this.
  • Visual Communication - Unlike wikipedia, a blog contains some visual representation such as images, symbols, animations, etc which increase the interest of the reader to go through it quickly making the blog seem more effective and interesting.

  • Design or Writting Structure - Provides an immediate well structured writing instead of just proving the blank page which decreases the workload.
  • Ideas - Initiates all the popular and impressive titles and information.

Types of Blogs

A Blog can be divided into many sub parts according to the interest and person. There are many different types of blogs depending upon the content within it, but also in the way that content is delivered.

1. Personal Blogs

It is a kind of blog where a person writes about the internal feelings, emotions, thoughts and experience they are going through. It is an ongoing online diary written by an individual rather than that of including a group or commeete. As it is written by an individual it does not attract a vast number of readers to go through it as it is not so family friendly. Even though a small number of users or authors have got recognition through this which made them popular, to that point they have attracted many other popular bloggers to sponsor them. Instead of the immediate or small circle few of the authors have gained significant recognition beyond their thinking.

2. Group Blogs

A type of weblog which engages a large number (two or more than two) of people within which posts are written and published by them. These blogs contain a lot of information in different sectors making the orientation of the blog very precise and authoritative. As it contains more people the organised form of the post seems effortless and provides the exact information. It is also known as collaborative blogs.

3. Organization Blogs

An institution involved in the making of the blog post for the betterment of the organisation. It shows the updated features and ongoing things within the organisation, also pointing out all the facilities and internal activities taking place. It can be private as well as public. In case of any company it will be kept private to maintain the democracy of employees but in vice versa organisations such as NGOs will keep an open record so that everyone can keep a track of that and help them in difficulty.

4. Video Blogs

The form of the blog which is a video log content which is first recorded and then delivered to the viewers to see. It basically contains all the videography content and does not have any written facts or information about it. It is the most used form in today's era as many people engage themselves in vlog for earning or sharing their life experience with the audience or viewers. It is also known as a modern blog.
