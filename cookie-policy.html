01. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, smartphone, tablet, or other device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, improve their performance, and provide information to website owners.

Think of cookies as small memory files for websites. They allow a website to "remember" your actions and preferences over time, so you don't have to re-enter information every time you visit the site or navigate between pages.

How Cookies Work

When you visit QuickGenAI, our web server may send a cookie to your browser, which stores it on your device. The next time you visit, your browser sends the cookie back to the server, allowing the website to recognize your browser and remember your preferences.

Cookie Duration

  • Session Cookies: Temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser, used to maintain your session during a single visit.
  • Persistent Cookies: Remain on your device for a set period or until manually deleted, used to remember preferences across multiple visits.
🔑 Cookies themselves do not contain personally identifiable information. They typically contain a unique identifier associated with an anonymous user profile that helps websites recognize returning visitors.

04. Essential Cookies

Essential cookies (also called "strictly necessary" cookies) are fundamental to the operation of QuickGenAI. Without these cookies, the website cannot function properly.

What Essential Cookies Do

  • Enable navigation between pages without losing context
  • Maintain the functionality of our AI tools during your session
  • Support form submissions and prevent duplicate submissions
  • Enable security features and fraud detection
  • Remember your cookie consent preference
  • Support technical load balancing across our servers

Can I Disable Essential Cookies?

Essential cookies cannot be disabled through cookie consent settings because they are fundamental to how the website works. They do not collect personal information for marketing or profiling purposes.

✅ Essential cookies are always active and do not require your consent under most privacy regulations, as they are strictly necessary for service delivery.

05. Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors interact with QuickGenAI, allowing us to improve the platform, fix issues, and provide a better experience for all users.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google LLC, which uses cookies to collect information about how visitors use our website. This includes pages visited, time spent, referral source, device/browser type, and approximate geographic region.

Google Analytics data is aggregated and anonymized. We have enabled IP anonymization, meaning your full IP address is never stored by Google Analytics.

Opting Out of Analytics

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on.

06. Advertising Cookies

QuickGenAI is a free platform, and advertising helps us sustain our services. Advertising cookies are used to deliver relevant advertisements to you on and off our platform.

  • Remember that you have visited our website

  • Limit how many times you see the same ad
  • Measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ad Settings or the Digital Advertising Alliance. Opting out does not stop ads — it means ads may be less relevant to you.

In addition to our own cookies, QuickGenAI works with various third-party services that may set their own cookies on your device, governed by those companies' own privacy and cookie policies.

  • Advertising Networks: Google AdSense and its advertising partners

08. Google AdSense & DoubleClick Cookies

QuickGenAI participates in the Google AdSense advertising program, which uses the DoubleClick cookie (and similar technologies) to serve ads based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

For more information, visit How Google Uses Information from Sites that Use Google Services. You can opt out at Google's Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

09. Managing & Disabling Cookies

You have full control over cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to view, delete, or block cookies, or browse in private/incognito mode.

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Privacy, search, and services → Cookies and site permissions
⚠️ Disabling cookies may affect the functionality of QuickGenAI and other websites you visit. Some features may not work correctly if cookies are disabled.

10. User Consent

When you first visit QuickGenAI, you may see a cookie consent banner informing you about our use of cookies and giving you the option to accept or decline non-essential cookies.

  • Accept All Cookies: You consent to all cookies, including analytics and advertising cookies.
  • Decline Non-Essential: Only essential cookies will be used; analytics and advertising cookies will be disabled.

You can withdraw or change your cookie consent at any time by clearing cookies in your browser settings and revisiting the site.

12. Cookie Policy FAQ

Are cookies dangerous or harmful?
No, cookies are not dangerous or harmful. They are simple text files and cannot carry viruses or malware, or access other files on your device. The main privacy concern is that they can track browsing behavior across websites, which is why we give you control over which cookies you accept.
Do I need to accept cookies to use QuickGenAI?
You do not need to accept all cookies. Essential cookies are always active since they're necessary for the website to function. You can decline non-essential cookies (analytics and advertising) and still use most of our tools and content.
Will I still see ads if I decline advertising cookies?
Yes, you may still see advertisements even if you decline advertising cookies — they'll just become generic rather than personalized to your interests. Ads are how we sustain our free platform.
How do I delete cookies that have already been set?
You can delete cookies through your browser settings — go to Settings or Preferences, find Privacy or Security, and look for options to clear cookies or browsing data.
Does QuickGenAI sell cookie data to third parties?
No. QuickGenAI does not sell your cookie data or any personal data to third parties. Advertising data is processed by Google AdSense under their own privacy policies to deliver relevant ads.
What is the difference between first-party and third-party cookies?
First-party cookies are set directly by QuickGenAI for essential functionality and our own analytics. Third-party cookies are set by external companies (like Google AdSense) whose services are integrated into our website.
