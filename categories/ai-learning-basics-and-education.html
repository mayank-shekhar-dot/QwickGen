About This Category

AI can feel overwhelming when you're just getting started — a flood of new tools, jargon, and "must-know" concepts appearing every week. This category exists to slow things down and explain AI the way it should be explained: in plain language, with real examples, and without assuming you already know what a "large language model" or "hallucination" means. Whether you're a student trying to use AI responsibly for coursework, a beginner writing your first prompt, or someone who just wants to understand what all the hype is actually about, these guides are built to take you from confused to confident, one concept at a time. We cover the fundamentals of how AI models actually work, common beginner mistakes and how to avoid them, safe and ethical ways to use AI tools in school or at work, and step-by-step tutorials for learning practical skills like coding with AI assistance. No prior technical background is required — just curiosity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a technical background to understand these articles?

No. Every article in this category is written for beginners first. We explain technical terms in plain language the first time they appear, and build up from basic concepts rather than assuming prior AI knowledge.

Is it safe for students to use AI for schoolwork?

It can be, if used responsibly. Our guides cover how to use AI as a learning aid rather than a shortcut — for example, using it to understand a concept rather than to write an assignment word-for-word — along with academic integrity considerations.

How often is this category updated?

We regularly add new beginner guides, tutorials, and explainers as AI tools and best practices evolve, so it's worth checking back for the latest additions.
