About This Category

The AI industry moves fast — a single week can bring a major product launch, a surprise layoff announcement, a new safety controversy, and a policy shift, often all at once. This category is our running coverage of what's actually happening in AI, without the hype or the panic. We track company moves from major players like OpenAI, Google, Meta, and Microsoft, break down what layoffs and restructuring announcements actually mean for the workforce, and explain emerging AI risks — from financial scams targeting vulnerable groups to security vulnerabilities being discovered in AI systems — in a way that's useful rather than alarmist. If you want to stay informed about where the AI industry is headed, understand the real-world impact of new AI regulations, or simply keep up with the news without wading through dozens of sources, this is where we bring it together in one place, updated as things develop.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you verify the AI news you publish?

We research each story from credible sources and link to them where relevant. Because AI news moves quickly, we always recommend cross-checking time-sensitive announcements (like company statements or product launches) with the original source.

Do you cover AI risks as well as AI achievements?

Yes. We believe balanced coverage means reporting both the exciting developments and the real risks — including safety concerns, misuse, job market impact, and regulatory issues — so you get the full picture.

Will this category help me understand how AI news affects my job or industry?

That's exactly what it's designed for. Many of our articles specifically break down what company restructuring, layoffs, or new AI regulations mean in practical terms for workers and businesses.
